# 📚 Pitemål Learning Guide
## A 4-Week Curriculum for the Piteå Dialect

---

## 🎯 Introduction

This guide is designed to take you from Standard Swedish to conversational Pitemål (Pite Bondska) in four phases. It aligns with the rules used in our **Pitemål Translator Engine**.

**Why Pitemål?**
Pitemål is one of the most distinct and well-preserved varieties of the Westrobothnian dialects (Bondska). It features unique grammar, vocabulary, and phonetic rules that differ significantly from Swedish.

---

## 🗓️ Phase 1: The Basics (Week 1)
**Focus:** Pronouns, Greetings, and Core Vocabulary

The quickest way to sound like a local is to switch your pronouns and common function words.

### Key Rules
1.  **I** instead of *jag*.
2.  **Dö** instead of *du*.
3.  **Ve** instead of *vi*.
4.  **Hä** instead of *det/den*.
5.  **Int** instead of *inte*.

### Practice
*   **Swedish:** "Jag heter Anna."
*   **Pitemål:** "I hät Anna."
*   **Swedish:** "Vi är här."
*   **Pitemål:** "Ve våra hänn."

---

## 🗣️ Phase 2: The Sound of Pite (Week 2)
**Focus:** Vowel Shifts and the "Thick L"

Pitemål has a deeper, more guttural sound.

### Vowel Shifts
*   **A → Å:** *Mat* becomes *måt*. *Kall* becomes *kåLL*.
*   **O → U:** *Bok* becomes *båok* or *buk*.

### The "Thick L" (Retroflex Flap)
Whenever you see **rd**, **rl**, or **rt** in Swedish, it often becomes a "Thick L" sound (written as **L** in our examples).
*   *Bord* (table) → **BoL**
*   *Hårt* (hard) → **HåLt**
*   *Farlig* (dangerous) → **FaLi**

---

## ✂️ Phase 3: Grammar & Apocope (Week 3)
**Focus:** Dropping vowels and changing verb forms.

### Apocope (The Chopping Block)
Pitemål speakers love to shorten words.
*   **Verbs:** Present tense verbs often lose their endings.
    *   *Dansar* → **Dans**
    *   *Springer* → **Spring**
    *   *Heter* → **Hät**
*   **Nouns:** Multi-syllable words often drop the final -a.
    *   *Skola* → **SkåoL**
    *   *Kasta* → **Kaast**

### Plural Verbs
Unlike Swedish, Pitemål conjugates verbs for plural subjects (we, you-pl, they).
*   **Singular:** *Han kåm* (He comes)
*   **Plural:** *Döm kåma* (They come) - Note the **-a** ending!

---

## 🎓 Phase 4: Immersion (Week 4+)
**Focus:** Complex sentences and flow.

Combine all previous rules. Use **"män"** for *men* (but) and **"wä"** for *med* (with).

### Sentence Building
**Swedish:** "Vi kommer från Sverige och talar språket."
1.  **Pronouns:** *Ve* (Vi)
2.  **Verbs:** *kåma* (plural come), *tåLa* (plural speak/talk)
3.  **Vocabulary:** *Schwerje* (Sverige), *språke* (språket - definite suffix change)
4.  **Conjunction:** *å* (och)

**Result:** "Ve kåma frå Schwerje å tåLa språke."
