🗣️ Bondska Alphabet & Pronunciation

The Bondska alphabet extends the standard Swedish alphabet to represent its unique vowel sounds.

Special Characters

  • Â / â: Represents a very fronted "a" sound (like in English "cat"). Common in Ume and Burträsk dialects.
  • Ô / ô: A sound between "ö" and "o" (often a schwa-like central vowel).
  • Å / å: Pronounced more open than in Swedish, often replacing standard "a".

Key Phonetic Features:

  • Vowel Shifts: Standard Swedish /o/ often becomes /u/ (bok → buk).
  • W Sound: The English /w/ sound is preserved in many words (var → wå or wars).
  • Retroflex Consonants: "Thick L" and retroflex "sh" (sj, skj) are prominent.
