💽 Sound Characteristics

  • Retroflex R: Deep, guttural sound - characteristic of Bondska
  • SK Sound: Becomes retroflexed [ʂ] - softer than standard Swedish
  • Long Vowels: Extended duration [Vː] in stressed syllables
  • Word-Final Consonants: Always pronounced (unlike Swedish)
  • Tonal Accents: Two pitch patterns distinguish word meanings
