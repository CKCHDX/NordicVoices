🗣️ Bondska Alphabet & Pronunciation

The Bondska alphabet uses the standard Swedish alphabet (A-Ö). The unique sounds of the dialect come from different pronunciations and sound combinations, as detailed in the project's ALPHABET.md file. The characters Â and Ô are not standard and have been removed.

Key Phonetic Features:

  • Vowel Shifts: Many Swedish vowels are pronounced differently (e.g., Swedish /o/ often becomes /u/).
  • Diphthongs: Combination of vowels to create gliding sounds not common in Standard Swedish.
  • Retroflex Consonants: Unique "thick" L and "sh" sounds (written as `sj`, `skj`, `stj`).

📚 Translation Logic

The translation process follows these steps:

  1. Normalization: The input text is converted to lowercase.
  2. Dictionary Lookup: Each word is checked against a dictionary of common Bondska words. If a match is found, it's replaced directly.
  3. Phonetic Rules: If a word is not in the dictionary, a series of basic phonetic rules are applied to approximate the Bondska pronunciation.

Phonetic Rules Applied:

  • a → å (e.g., mat → måt)
  • e → ä (e.g., se → sä)
  • o → u (e.g., bok → buk)
