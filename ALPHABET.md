# 🗣️ Pitemål Alphabet & Phonetic Guide
## Reference for Piteå Dialect (Pitemål)

The Pitemål alphabet is based on the standard Swedish alphabet but adds specific notation to represent its unique retroflex and vowel sounds.

---

## 📝 Writing Rules (Orthography)

### 1. Apocope (Vowel Dropping)
Pitemål is characterized by **apocope**, meaning final vowels are often dropped in multi-syllable words.
*   *kasta* (throw) → **kaast**
*   *skola* (school) → **skåoL**
*   *dansa* (dance) → **dåns**

### 2. Definite Articles
The definite article is suffixed differently than in Swedish.
*   **-en** often becomes **-n** (or **-a** for feminine nouns).
    *   *bilen* → **biLn**
*   **-et** often becomes **-e**.
    *   *huset* → **huse**

### 3. Plural Articles
The definite plural "the" (*de/dem*) is often represented as **dii** or distinguished from the subject pronoun **döm** (they).

### 4. Thick L Notation
In this project, we use a capital **L** in the middle of words to denote the "Thick L" sound (retroflex flap), which typically replaces *rd*, *rl*, or *rt* in Swedish words.
*   *bord* → **boL**
*   *farlig* → **faLi**
